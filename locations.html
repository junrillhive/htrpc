Regional cities and telehealth

Patients across South Hampton Roads choose our Portsmouth practice for shorter wait times, direct clinician access, and coordinated hybrid care.

Norfolk, VA Primary Care

Just across the Elizabeth River, patients from Downtown Norfolk, Ghent, Freemason, Berkley, Campostella, and Naval Station Norfolk reach 355 Crawford Street in 8 to 15 minutes via the Downtown Tunnel (I-264 W) or Midtown Tunnel (US-58 W). Prefer to skip tunnel traffic? Board the HRT Elizabeth River Pedestrian Ferry at Norfolk’s Waterside Marina and step off at High Street Landing · one short block from our lobby.

Chesapeake, VA Primary Care

Chesapeake families in Western Branch, Bowers Hill, Deep Creek, South Norfolk, Camelot, and Greenbrier are only 10 to 18 minutes from Suite 622 via I-264 E, George Washington Highway (US-17 N), or I-464 N · often without crossing a single toll tunnel from Western Branch or Deep Creek. We accept Anthem, Sentara/Optima, Medicare, Medicaid, and TRICARE for Chesapeake residents.

How Hybrid In-Clinic & Telemedicine Care Works

Whether you are commuting through Hampton Roads bridge-tunnel traffic, caring for an aging parent at home, or traveling elsewhere in the Commonwealth of Virginia, our encrypted OptiMantra video platform keeps you connected to the exact same Portsmouth care team.

Ideal Telehealth Visits

Lab & imaging result reviews, blood pressure and A1C check-ins, medication refills, anxiety/depression follow-ups, and acute sinus, allergy, skin, or cold evaluations.

Local Pharmacy E-Prescribing

Prescriptions are transmitted electronically during your video visit to your preferred CVS, Walgreens, Walmart, Kroger, Harris Teeter, or military pharmacy.
