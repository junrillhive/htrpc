How We Personalize Your Care Plan

Whether you were just diagnosed with high blood pressure or prediabetes, want to quit smoking for good, or are helping an aging parent manage ten prescriptions, we equip you with clear, written action plans.

Smoking & Nicotine Cessation Program

Personalized quit plans combining behavioral trigger counseling with FDA-approved nicotine replacement therapy (patches, gum, lozenges), varenicline, or bupropion.

New Diagnosis & Lab Literacy Sessions

Plain-English walkthroughs of hypertension, Type 2 diabetes, atrial fibrillation, high cholesterol, or kidney stage, ncluding how to log home cuff and glucose readings.

Medication Adherence & Simplification

Teaching what each pill does, timing doses to prevent side effects, setting up 7-day pill organizers, and switching to affordable generic or 90-day fills.

Stroke, Cardiac & Senior Caregiver Guidance

B.E. F.A.S.T. stroke recognition, heart failure daily weight tracking, fall-prevention strategies, and support for family caregivers across Hampton Roads.

Frequently asked questions

Answers from our Portsmouth clinical team about one-on-one health coaching, insurance coverage, and scheduling.

Explore All 12 Services
What does health education and counseling encompass at your clinic?

Our counseling sessions cover understanding new diagnoses and lab panels, medication management and side-effect prevention, smoking and vaping cessation, heart and stroke risk reduction, exercise safety, and senior caregiver guidance.

Can you help me quit smoking or vaping with prescription support?

Yes! Quitting tobacco with clinician counseling and medical therapy (such as combination nicotine replacement or varenicline) more than doubles your success rate compared to quitting cold turkey.

Can a family member or caregiver join my health education session?

Absolutely. With your permission, spouses, adult children, or caregivers are welcome to join in-person at Suite 622 or on a three-way Virginia telemedicine video call so everyone is aligned on your care plan.

Where can I read your clinician-reviewed guides between appointments?

Our complete Patient Education Hub at htrpc.com/education/ is free and open 24/7, featuring interactive blood pressure and A1C target checkers and 8 condition guides.
