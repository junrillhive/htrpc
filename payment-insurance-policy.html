What documents are accepted to qualify for the Sliding Fee Scale?

To determine eligibility for Categories A through E (or +$5,680 per additional household member beyond 8), please bring one of the following documents showing gross household income: (1) Form 1040 U.S. Income Tax Return for the prior calendar year; (2) Social Services Notice of Action for Food Stamp Allotment showing gross monthly income; (3) Social Security Benefit Statement or SSA Award Letter; or (4) Virginia Employment Commission (VEC) Form DG90 or DG91.

What are my rights under the Federal Good Faith Estimate law?

Under the federal No Surprises Act, patients who do not have insurance or who are not using insurance have the right to receive a written Good Faith Estimate explaining how much their medical care will cost before scheduled services are provided. You can request a Good Faith Estimate for any non-emergency visit or lab service by calling (757) 516-7554.

When are copayments, deductibles, and sliding fees due?

Insurance copayments, coinsurance, and sliding-scale nominal fees ($25–$65) are collected at the time of check-in at Suite 622 or prior to your Virginia telemedicine visit. We accept Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express, HSA/FSA cards, and personal checks.

Are Diagnostic Labs or Quest/LabCorp tests included in the office visit fee?

Your office visit fee covers your evaluation and care plan with our clinician. When outside laboratory panels or imaging studies are needed, we route orders to your in-network laboratory (Quest Diagnostics, LabCorp, or Bon Secours / Sentara) or arrange discounted client-bill pricing for self-pay patients.
