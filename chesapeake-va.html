Frequently asked questions

Answers for patients visiting from Chesapeake, VA regarding driving directions, parking, insurance, and hybrid telehealth.

All 6 Service Areas
Do I have to cross any toll tunnels to reach your clinic from Western Branch or Deep Creek?

No. Because Portsmouth sits on the same side of the Elizabeth River as Western Branch and Deep Creek, driving to 355 Crawford Street via I-264 East or George Washington Highway (US-17 North) requires zero tunnel crossings and zero tunnel tolls.

How long does it take to drive from Western Branch or Greenbrier in Chesapeake?

From Western Branch (23321) or South Norfolk (23324), the drive is only 10 to 13 minutes. From Deep Creek (23323) it is 12 to 15 minutes, and from Greenbrier (23320) it is 16 to 19 minutes via I-64 W and I-464 N.

Can you send prescriptions to my local pharmacy in Chesapeake?

Yes. Whether you visit us in person at Suite 622 or connect via Virginia Telehealth, we e-prescribe directly to your preferred Chesapeake pharmacy in Western Branch, Greenbrier, Deep Creek, or Great Bridge.

What insurance plans do you accept for Chesapeake patients?

We accept Medicare, Medicare Advantage, Virginia Medicaid, Sentara Health Plans (Optima), Anthem BlueCross BlueShield, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, and TRICARE, plus our $25–$65 sliding-scale fee schedule for uninsured households.
