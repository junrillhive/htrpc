How Your Body Repairs Itself

At 355 Crawford Street, Suite 622 and via Virginia Telehealth, we build a structured 12-week quit plan around your daily routine, stress triggers, and medical history.

Dual Nicotine Replacement (NRT)

A single patch or gum alone often leaves breakthrough cravings. We pair a steady 24-hour nicotine patch (for baseline withdrawal control) with rapid-acting gum, lozenges, or nasal spray for morning or post-meal urges.

Prescription Varenicline & Bupropion

Varenicline (Chantix generic) blocks nicotine receptors while gently easing withdrawal, even allowing a gradual “reduce-to-quit” start week. Bupropion SR reduces cravings while helping prevent post-quit weight gain.

Vaping & Smokeless Tobacco Plans

High-concentration salt-nicotine vapes and pouches often deliver more daily nicotine than a pack of cigarettes. We calculate your exact milligram intake and step you down smoothly without brain fog.

Annual Low-Dose CT Lung Screening

For adults aged 50 to 80 with a 20+ pack-year smoking history who currently smoke or quit within the past 15 years, we order annual low-dose CT lung scans·covered at $0 copay by Medicare and most plans.

All 8 Clinical Guides
Does insurance cover prescription quit-smoking medications and nicotine patches?

Yes. Under the Affordable Care Act, Medicare Part B/D, Virginia Medicaid, and TRICARE, tobacco cessation counseling visits and FDA-approved pharmacotherapy (including varenicline, bupropion, and prescribed nicotine patches, gum, or lozenges) are covered, often with $0 out-of-pocket copay when prescribed by your primary care provider.

What if I have tried quitting multiple times before and relapsed?

Most former smokers tried 5 to 7 times before quitting permanently. A past slip is never a failure·it tells us which triggers (morning coffee, work stress, driving, or under-dosed nicotine replacement) need a stronger medical buffer this time.

How can I avoid gaining weight when I quit smoking?

Nicotine slightly speeds up baseline metabolism and blunts appetite, so it is common to crave crunchy or sweet snacks during the first month. We can pair your quit plan with bupropion SR or GLP-1/nutrition strategies, sugar-free lozenges, high-protein snacks, and brisk daily walks to keep your weight steady.

Who qualifies for a Low-Dose CT Lung Screening?

The USPSTF and Medicare cover an annual Low-Dose CT (LDCT) scan of the chest for adults aged 50 to 80 who have at least a 20 pack-year smoking history (for example, 1 pack a day for 20 years, or half a pack a day for 40 years) and either still smoke or quit within the last 15 years.
