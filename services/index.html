Preventive and primary care

Routine wellness exams, early-detection lab panels, adult immunizations, and convenient virtual visits designed to keep you ahead of health concerns.

Annual Wellness Exams

Preventive Foundation · Covered at $0 Copay on Most Plans

Build a strong foundation of health with annual wellness exams structured to promote early detection and long-term prevention. During your visit at Suite 622, your clinician reviews your complete medical and family history, current medications, vital signs, lifestyle habits, and personalized screening schedule.

What Is Included

Full physical exam, vital signs, medication safety reconciliation, lab orders, and preventive roadmap

Who Should Schedule

Adults of all ages establishing primary care in Portsmouth or completing yearly Medicare Wellness visits

Book Annual Wellness Exam First Visit Checklist

Preventive Health Screenings

Proactive Detection · Cardiovascular & Metabolic Panels

Preventive health screenings identify silent risk factors long before symptoms appear, when treatment is simplest and most effective. Depending on your age and history, we coordinate blood pressure checks, lipid and cholesterol panels, hemoglobin A1C diabetes testing, cardiovascular risk assessments, and age-appropriate cancer screenings.

Core Screenings

Blood pressure, fasting lipid profile, A1C glucose, thyroid & metabolic panels, and cancer checks

Related Guides

Heart Disease Prevention · Blood Pressure Guide

Schedule Health Screening

Immunizations & Vaccinations

Immunity & Protection · Adult & Family Boosters

Safeguard your health and fortify the Hampton Roads community with timely immunizations tailored to your age, occupation, travel plans, and medical history. Our clinical team reviews your vaccination record and administers seasonal flu shots, tetanus-diphtheria-pertussis (Tdap) boosters, shingles, pneumonia, and preventive adult vaccines.

Vaccines Offered

Seasonal influenza, Tdap/Td, Shingrix (shingles), pneumococcal, and routine adult boosters

Insurance Coverage

Covered at 100% under Medicare Part B/D, Medicaid, TRICARE, and commercial preventive benefits

Book Vaccine Appointment

Telemedicine Consultations

Virtual Primary Care · Anywhere in Virginia

Connect with your Portsmouth provider from home or work anywhere in the Commonwealth of Virginia. Telemedicine consultations remove transportation and scheduling barriers for medication refills, lab result reviews, chronic condition check-ins, mental health follow-ups, and minor acute illnesses.

Ideal For

Prescription refills, lab & imaging review, hypertension/diabetes check-ins, and anxiety/depression care

Service Area

Portsmouth, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Suffolk, Virginia Beach, and all of Virginia

Chronic and acute care

Structured long-term care for complex conditions paired with same-week office visits for sudden illnesses, minor injuries, and mental wellness.

Chronic Disease Management

Long-Term Continuity · Cardiology & Internal Medicine Depth

Managing diabetes, high blood pressure, heart failure, atrial fibrillation, high cholesterol, asthma, or COPD requires consistent monitoring and a clinician who knows your history. We build individualized care plans combining evidence-based medication management, quarterly lab tracking, and practical lifestyle adjustments.

Conditions Managed

Hypertension, Type 2 diabetes, heart disease, heart failure, AFib, hyperlipidemia, asthma & thyroid disorders

Acute Illness Care

Same-Week Sick Visits · Skip Urgent Care Waits

When sudden illness strikes, prompt evaluation from your own primary care team gets you feeling better faster without crowded urgent care delays. We reserve daily weekday appointment slots at Suite 622 for colds, influenza, strep throat, sinus and ear infections, bronchitis, urinary tract infections, rashes, and stomach illnesses.

Conditions Treated

Flu, strep, sinusitis, ear infections, upper respiratory infections, UTIs, gastrointestinal bugs & skin rashes

Fast Scheduling

Call (757) 516-7554 or book online for same-day or next-day evaluation

Book Same-Week Sick Visit

Minor Injury Treatment

Emergency Medicine & Navy IDC Experience

Led by clinicians with emergency department and U.S. Navy Special Operations Independent Duty Corpsman backgrounds, our clinic treats non-emergency injuries promptly in Downtown Portsmouth. We provide wound care, superficial cut and abrasion dressing, minor burn treatment, and joint sprain or muscle strain evaluation.

Injuries Treated

Sprains, muscle strains, superficial cuts & abrasions, minor thermal burns, and wound follow-up

Why Primary Care

Lower primary care copay and continuity with the clinicians who already know your medical history

Management of Mental Health Conditions

Confidential, Trauma-Informed Support

Mental health is inseparable from physical well-being. We provide a safe, judgment-free sanctuary for evaluating and managing anxiety, depression, stress-related disorders, and sleep difficulties through customized treatment plans, medication management, lifestyle counseling, and coordinated behavioral referrals.

Care Approach

Trauma-informed assessment, evidence-based medication management, and whole-person stress & sleep support

Flexible Follow-Up

Available in-person at Suite 622 or via private Virginia telemedicine video sessions

Women’s Health Services

Every Stage of Adult Life · Portsmouth, Norfolk, Chesapeake & Suffolk

Your health needs evolve over time, and having a trusted provider makes all the difference. Our women’s health services include annual well-woman exams, routine preventive screenings, reproductive and contraceptive health support, thyroid and bone density evaluations, and compassionate perimenopause and menopause management.

Services Offered

Well-woman physicals, cervical & breast screening coordination, hormonal & menopause care

Continuity of Care

Integrated with cardiovascular, metabolic, and mental health support under one roof

Nutrition & Weight Management Advice

Food-Based Metabolic Medicine · No Referral Required

Achieve sustainable health with evidence-based nutrition and medical weight management tailored to your metabolism and medical history. Our clinicians emphasize practical, food-based strategies to lower A1C, reduce blood pressure, improve cholesterol, and support healthy weight loss without fad diets.

What We Address

Metabolic syndrome, prediabetes, Type 2 diabetes nutrition, DASH heart-healthy eating & weight management
