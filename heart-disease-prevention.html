Lipids & cardiac screening

Total cholesterol alone tells only half the story. Here is how we evaluate every fraction of your lipid panel and vascular risk profile.

Cardiac Prevention

Preventive Cardiology · Reviewed by Laurie A. Salerno, ANP-BC

Every 40 mg/dL reduction in LDL cholesterol lowers the risk of heart attack and ischemic stroke by roughly 22%.

Beyond Total Cholesterol

At Hampton Roads Primary Care, we interpret your fasting lipid panel alongside your blood pressure, Hemoglobin A1C, family history, and 10-year Atherosclerotic Cardiovascular Disease (ASCVD) score.

LDL & Non-HDL Cholesterol Goals

LDL (“bad”) cholesterol carries plaque into artery walls. We aim for LDL under 100 mg/dL for general prevention, and under 70 mg/dL (or < 55 mg/dL) for patients with diabetes or known heart disease.

Triglycerides & Insulin Sensitivity

Fasting triglycerides above 150 mg/dL are closely tied to excess dietary sugar, refined carbohydrates, and insulin resistance rather than dietary cholesterol alone.

Soluble Fiber & Mediterranean Fats

Oats, barley, beans, chia seeds, walnuts, fatty fish (salmon, sardines), and extra-virgin olive oil bind bile acids in the gut and naturally lower circulating LDL by 10% to 15%.

Personalized Statin & Non-Statin Care

When medication is indicated to stabilize arterial plaque, we choose well-tolerated doses, monitor liver enzymes, and offer ezetimibe or alternative regimens if muscle sensitivity occurs.

Frequently asked questions

Evidence-based answers from our Portsmouth primary care clinicians regarding heart disease prevention, lab testing, and follow-up care.

All 8 Clinical Guides
How long should I fast before my cholesterol blood draw?

For the most accurate triglyceride and calculated LDL measurement, fast for 9 to 12 hours before your morning appointment·drinking plenty of plain water and taking your regular morning blood pressure pills unless instructed otherwise.

Why would my doctor recommend a statin if my cholesterol is only mildly elevated?

Statins do more than lower LDL numbers: they calm arterial inflammation and harden soft, vulnerable plaque so it cannot rupture and cause a heart attack or stroke. If you have diabetes, hypertension, or an elevated 10-year ASCVD risk score, plaque stabilization provides major protection even at moderate LDL levels.

What if I had muscle aches on a cholesterol medication in the past?

True statin-related muscle soreness occurs in about 5% to 10% of patients and is almost always reversible. At Suite 622, we check Vitamin D and thyroid levels (which often mimic muscle aches), switch to a hydrophilic statin like rosuvastatin or pravastatin at a low or every-other-day dose, or use non-statin options like ezetimibe.

Does eating eggs raise my blood cholesterol as much as saturated fat?

For most people, saturated and trans fats (found in fatty red meats, butter, fried foods, and pastries) raise blood LDL cholesterol far more than dietary cholesterol in eggs. Enjoying eggs in moderation alongside oatmeal, vegetables, olive oil, and fish fits well within a heart-protective diet.
