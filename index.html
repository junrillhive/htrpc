Are you accepting new patients in Portsmouth, VA?

Yes. Hampton Roads Primary Care welcomes new adult and family patients from Portsmouth, Norfolk, Chesapeake, and Suffolk. You can book online through our OptiMantra portal or call (757) 516-7554 for a same-week appointment.

Which health insurance plans do you accept?

We accept Virginia Medicare, Virginia Medicaid, TRICARE, Anthem BlueCross BlueShield, and Sentara Health Plans. For uninsured or underinsured patients, we offer a Sliding-Scale Fee Program and a written Good Faith Estimate before care begins.

Can I schedule a same-day sick visit or urgent appointment?

Yes. We reserve daily appointment slots Monday through Friday (8:00 AM – 5:00 PM) for acute illnesses such as flu, strep throat, sinus infections, UTIs, rashes, and minor sprains or lacerations.

Who will I see during my appointment at Suite 622?

You will be cared for by our board-certified clinicians: Aaron Sents, PA-C (Yale University MMS, 10-year U.S. Navy Special Operations Independent Duty Corpsman), Laurie A. Salerno, ANP-BC (34 years in nursing, internal medicine, and cardiology), or Shanice Edwards, NP-BC (Family Nurse Practitioner).

Do you offer telemedicine appointments across Virginia?

Yes. Patients anywhere in Virginia can schedule HIPAA-compliant video consultations for medication refills, lab reviews, mental health follow-ups, chronic disease check-ins, and minor acute illnesses.

Where is the clinic located and what should I bring?

We are located at 355 Crawford Street, Suite 622, Portsmouth, VA 23704, on the Downtown Portsmouth waterfront. Please bring a photo ID, your current insurance card (or income verification if applying for the sliding scale), and a list or bottles of your current medications.
