Frequently asked questions

Answers for patients visiting from Portsmouth, VA regarding driving directions, parking, insurance, and hybrid telehealth.

All 6 Service Areas
Where do I park when visiting 355 Crawford Street, Suite 622 in Portsmouth?

Convenient metered street parking is available directly in front of the building on Crawford Street and along High Street. Covered public parking is also available one block away at the Water Street Parking Garage and the County Street Garage. Our building features a ground-level ADA entrance and direct elevators to the 6th Floor, Suite 622.

Do you accept TRICARE for families near Naval Medical Center Portsmouth and the Shipyard?

Yes. Led by Aaron Sents, PA-C (a 10-year U.S. Navy Special Operations Independent Duty Corpsman), we proudly accept TRICARE Prime, TRICARE Select, TRICARE For Life, and CHAMPVA for active-duty families, Navy retirees, and Norfolk Naval Shipyard civil service personnel.

How does the $25–$65 Sliding-Scale Fee Program work for Portsmouth residents?

Because Portsmouth is a designated Medically Underserved Area (MUA), uninsured and underinsured residents whose household income falls at or below 200% of the 2026 Federal Poverty Guidelines qualify for flat primary care visits capped at $25, $35, $45, $55, or $65 based on household size and income.

Can I get a same-week sick visit or annual physical in Portsmouth?

Yes. We reserve daily appointment blocks at Suite 622 for Portsmouth patients who need same-week acute illness evaluations, blood pressure or diabetes follow-ups, or new patient annual wellness exams.
