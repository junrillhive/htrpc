Confidential Primary Care Support · Anxiety, Depression & Sleep

Available in-person at 355 Crawford Street, Suite 622 in Downtown Portsmouth and supported across Hampton Roads.

How We Personalize Your Care Plan

Because physical conditions like thyroid disorders, Vitamin D/B12 deficiency, anemia, and hormonal shifts frequently mimic anxiety or depression, treating mental wellness inside primary care gives you a complete diagnostic picture.

Generalized Anxiety & Panic Support

Validated GAD-7 assessments, non-controlled first-line pharmacotherapy (SSRIs, SNRIs, buspirone), breathing and grounding strategies, and regular follow-up check-ins.

Depression & Mood Management

Compassionate evaluation of persistent sadness, loss of interest, grief, and seasonal mood shifts with evidence-based medication management and therapy coordination.

Insomnia, Burnout & Caregiver Stress

Evaluation of sleep-onset and sleep-maintenance insomnia, obstructive sleep apnea screening, circadian sleep hygiene protocols, and support for military and family caregivers.

Medical & Metabolic Lab Screening

Targeted blood panels (TSH, Free T4, Vitamin B12, Vitamin D, ferritin, iron, and CMP) to rule out underlying physiological causes of fatigue, brain fog, or palpitations.

Frequently asked questions

Answers from our Portsmouth clinical team about integrated mind-body care, insurance coverage, and scheduling.

Explore All 12 Services
What mental health conditions can be managed by my primary care provider?

We evaluate and manage mild-to-moderate depression, generalized anxiety disorder, social anxiety, panic symptoms, insomnia, adjustment disorders, caregiver burnout, and stress-related physical symptoms.

How does starting with primary care help with anxiety or depression?

Wait times are much shorter than specialty psychiatry, you meet with a clinician you already trust, and we check medical labs (like thyroid and vitamin levels) simultaneously to ensure nothing physical is being overlooked.

Do you coordinate with counselors, psychologists, or psychiatrists when needed?

Yes. Medication and lifestyle care work best alongside talk therapy. We gladly coordinate referrals to trusted Hampton Roads behavioral health therapists and psychiatric specialists whenever specialized care is indicated.

Can I do my mental health follow-up visits by telemedicine?

Yes! Once your care plan is established, Virginia telemedicine video visits are a convenient, private way to check in on medication comfort and symptom progress.
