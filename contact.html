Scheduling and contact FAQ

Quick answers on same-week appointments, prescription refills, and after-hours guidance.

Read First Visit Checklist
How quickly can I get a new patient or sick visit appointment?

We reserve daily slots for acute sick visits and same-week openings for new patient physicals and chronic care evaluations. Call (757) 516-7554 at 8:00 AM for same-day triage or book online 24/7 via OptiMantra.

How do I request a prescription refill?

Established patients can request medication refills through the OptiMantra Patient Portal or ask their pharmacy to send an electronic refill request. Please allow 24 to 48 business hours for routine refills.

What is your cancellation or rescheduling policy?

If you need to reschedule or switch an in-person visit to a Virginia telemedicine video appointment, please call (757) 516-7554 at least 24 hours in advance so we can offer the slot to another neighbor in need.

What should I do if I have an urgent medical issue after 5:00 PM?

If you experience a life-threatening emergency such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke symptoms, or severe bleeding, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room (such as Bon Secours Maryview Medical Center or Sentara Norfolk General Hospital). For non-urgent needs after hours, message us in the Patient Portal or book online for the next business morning.
