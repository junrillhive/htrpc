Frequently asked questions

Answers for patients visiting from Virginia Telehealth regarding driving directions, parking, insurance, and hybrid telehealth.

All 6 Service Areas
Who is eligible for a Virginia Telehealth visit with Hampton Roads Primary Care?

Any adult or family physically located within the Commonwealth of Virginia at the time of the video consultation is eligible. This includes residents across Hampton Roads, the Peninsula, Richmond, Northern Virginia, and rural Virginia communities.

Do I need to download special software for my video appointment?

No. Prior to your appointment, you will receive a secure text message and email link through our HIPAA-compliant OptiMantra portal. Clicking the link opens your encrypted video visit directly in Safari, Chrome, or Edge on your smartphone, tablet, or computer.

Can you prescribe medications during a Virginia Telehealth visit?

Yes. Our clinicians can prescribe and refill blood pressure, diabetes, cholesterol, thyroid, asthma, mental health (SSRIs/SNRIs), and acute illness medications electronically to any pharmacy in Virginia. (Note: DEA-scheduled controlled substances cannot be initiated via telehealth alone.)

Are Virginia Telehealth visits covered by Medicare, Medicaid, TRICARE, and commercial insurance?

Yes. Telemedicine primary care visits are covered by Medicare, Virginia Medicaid, TRICARE, Sentara Health Plans, Anthem BCBS, Aetna, Cigna, and UnitedHealthcare, and uninsured Virginia residents qualify for our $25–$65 sliding-scale fee schedule.
