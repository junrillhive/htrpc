Frequently asked questions

Answers for patients visiting from Suffolk, VA regarding driving directions, parking, insurance, and hybrid telehealth.

All 6 Service Areas
How far is Hampton Roads Primary Care from Harbour View and Northern Suffolk?

Our clinic at 355 Crawford Street, Suite 622 is just 9.6 miles (14 to 16 minutes) east of Harbour View via the Western Freeway (I-164 East) to London Boulevard, with no tunnels or tolls.

Can Suffolk patients combine in-office physicals with telehealth follow-ups?

Yes. Many patients in Downtown Suffolk, Chuckatuck, and Whaleyville visit Suite 622 once a year for their comprehensive wellness exam and lab draw, and use our HIPAA-compliant Virginia Telehealth platform for quarterly blood pressure, A1C, and prescription follow-ups.

Do you accept Medicare, TRICARE, and Sentara Health Plans for Suffolk residents?

Yes. We accept Original Medicare, Medicare Advantage, TRICARE Prime/Select/For Life, Virginia Medicaid, Sentara Health Plans (Optima), Anthem BCBS, Aetna, Cigna, and UnitedHealthcare, as well as our $25–$65 sliding-scale fee program.

How quickly can a new patient from Suffolk get an appointment?

Unlike large health-system clinics in Northern Suffolk that often have 6-to-12-week waits for new patients, we offer same-week new patient wellness exams and chronic care appointments.
