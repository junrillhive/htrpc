Neighborhood Corridors & Arrival Directions

355 Crawford Street sits directly opposite Norfolk’s Waterside District on the Elizabeth River. Many Downtown Norfolk and Freemason professionals walk onto the HRT Ferry during their lunch hour and step off at High Street Landing one block from our front door.

Downtown Norfolk, Freemason & Waterside (23510)

Take I-264 W through the Downtown Tunnel to Exit 7 (High Street / Downtown Portsmouth) in 6 to 8 minutes, or board the HRT Elizabeth River Pedestrian Ferry at Waterside Marina for a 5-minute crossing to High Street Landing.

Ghent, West Ghent, Chelsea & EVMS Corridor (23507)

Take Hampton Boulevard south to the Midtown Tunnel (US-58 W), exit onto London Boulevard eastbound, and turn right onto Crawford Street in 8 to 10 minutes.

Larchmont, ODU, Colonial Place & Lafayette (23508 / 23509)

Drive south on Hampton Boulevard to the Midtown Tunnel (US-58 W) for a 12-to-14-minute trip to Downtown Portsmouth, or schedule a HIPAA video visit from home.

Naval Station Norfolk, Ocean View & Berkley (23511 / 23523)

From Berkley and Campostella, cross via I-264 W in 7 minutes. From Naval Station Norfolk and Ocean View, take I-564 W to Hampton Blvd / Midtown Tunnel (18–22 minutes) or use TRICARE-covered telehealth.

Frequently asked questions

Answers for patients visiting from Norfolk, VA regarding driving directions, parking, insurance, and hybrid telehealth.

All 6 Service Areas
How close is your clinic to the HRT Elizabeth River Ferry from Norfolk?

Our building at 355 Crawford Street sits just one short block from the High Street Ferry Landing (North Landing) in Downtown Portsmouth. You can board the pedestrian ferry at Waterside in Downtown Norfolk, enjoy a 5-minute ride across the Elizabeth River, and walk to our lobby in under 2 minutes.

Which tunnel is faster when driving from Norfolk to 355 Crawford Street?

From Downtown Norfolk, Freemason, Berkley, or East Beach, the I-264 West Downtown Tunnel to Exit 7 (High Street) is fastest (6–10 minutes). From Ghent, West Ghent, ODU, Larchmont, or Naval Station Norfolk, the US-58 West Midtown Tunnel to London Boulevard East is the most direct route (8–15 minutes).

Can Norfolk patients use Virginia Telehealth to avoid tunnel tolls on follow-up visits?

Absolutely. Many of our Norfolk patients come to Suite 622 once or twice a year for their comprehensive physical and blood draw, and complete all blood pressure, diabetes, mental health, and medication refill follow-ups via HIPAA-compliant video visits from home or work in Norfolk.

Do you accept Sentara Health Plans (Optima), Anthem, Medicare, and TRICARE for Norfolk residents?

Yes. We are in-network with Medicare, Medicare Advantage, Virginia Medicaid, Sentara Health Plans / Optima, Anthem BlueCross BlueShield, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, and TRICARE, and we also offer our $25–$65 sliding-scale fee program.
