Meet our medical team

Our three board-certified clinicians bring Yale University training, U.S. Navy Special Operations medicine, 34 years of cardiac and internal medicine, and family-centered nursing to Portsmouth.

Aaron Sents, PA-C

Physician Assistant-Certified · Accepting New Patients

Aaron Sents, PA-C earned his Master of Medical Science degree from Yale University in 2022 after completing his Bachelor of Science in Health Sciences (Public Health) at Old Dominion University in 2019. His career as a Physician Assistant began in Emergency Medicine working full-time at Bon Secours Maryview Medical Center in Portsmouth, where he continues to practice part-time.

His clinical foundation was forged during a ten-year career in the U.S. Navy, culminating as a Special Operations Independent Duty Corpsman (IDC) with the Marine Forces Special Operations Command (MARSOC) at Camp Lejeune, NC. By implementing the Hampton Roads Primary Care vision, he equips patients through education to enhance their physical, mental, and spiritual well-being.

Education & Service

MMS, Yale University (2022) · BS Public Health, ODU (2019) · 10-Yr U.S. Navy Special Ops IDC

Clinical Focus

Adult Primary Care, Veteran & TRICARE Medicine, Acute Illness, Minor Injury & Preventive Care

Laurie Salerno, ANP-BC

Adult Nurse Practitioner-Board Certified · Accepting New Patients

Laurie A. Salerno, ANP-BC earned her Master of Science degree in Adult Nurse Practitioner studies from the State University of New York at Buffalo, where she conducted clinical research on cardiac rehabilitation for heart failure patients, following her undergraduate nursing degree at D’Youville College in Buffalo, NY.

Laurie brings 34 years of clinical experience · 17 years as a registered nurse and 17 years as a nurse practitioner, the majority spent at Buffalo General Medical Center where she helped establish an outpatient heart and lung center for medical students and residents while maintaining a private internal medicine and cardiology practice for 12 years. She emphasizes practical, food-based nutrition for diabetes, hypertension, and heart disease, and has served on medical missions to Haiti and Nicaragua.

Education & Experience

MS, SUNY Buffalo · BSN, D’Youville College · 34 Years in Internal Medicine & Cardiac Nursing

Clinical Focus

Cardiology, Heart Failure, Hypertension, Type 2 Diabetes & Food-Based Clinical Nutrition

Careers and clinic FAQ

Hampton Roads Primary Care is located in a federally designated Medically Underserved Area (MUA) in Portsmouth, VA. Qualified clinicians may be eligible for state and federal student loan repayment programs.

Now Hiring Primary Care Providers

We are seeking compassionate Primary Care Physicians, Nurse Practitioners, and Physician Assistants dedicated to preventive medicine, chronic care, and clinical preceptor leadership. Send your CV to info@htrpc.com.

What makes Hampton Roads Primary Care different from hospital chains?

As an independent Portsmouth practice at 355 Crawford Street, Suite 622, we schedule unhurried appointments where you see the same three clinicians · Aaron Sents, PA-C, Laurie Salerno, ANP-BC, and Shanice Edwards, NP-BC · without rotating through dozens of unfamiliar providers.

Are all three clinicians currently accepting new patients?

Yes. Aaron Sents, PA-C, Laurie A. Salerno, ANP-BC, and Shanice Edwards, NP-BC are all actively accepting new adult and family patients from Portsmouth, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Suffolk, and Virginia Beach.

How does your Medically Underserved Area (MUA) mission help patients?

Our commitment to Portsmouth means no patient is turned away due to inability to pay. Alongside Medicare, Medicaid, TRICARE, Anthem, and Sentara, we provide an income-based Sliding-Scale Fee Program ($25–$65 per visit) with written Good Faith Estimates.

How do I apply for a clinical position at Hampton Roads Primary Care?

Physicians, Nurse Practitioners, Physician Assistants, and medical support professionals can email their resume and cover letter to info@htrpc.com or call (757) 516-7554.
