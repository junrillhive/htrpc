How We Personalize Your Care Plan

We look beneath the scale to understand your insulin resistance, thyroid function, sleep quality, medications (some of which cause weight gain), and family food traditions.

Metabolic & Hormone Lab Baseline

Comprehensive blood panel checking Hemoglobin A1C, fasting insulin/glucose, lipid panel, liver enzymes (NAFLD screen), TSH thyroid panel, and kidney function.

Practical Grocery & Plate Coaching

Anti-inflammatory, DASH, and low-glycemic meal templates using affordable everyday groceries, ocusing on protein adequacy, fiber timing, and hidden sodium/sugar swaps.

Medical Weight-Loss Pharmacotherapy

When clinically indicated alongside nutrition, we evaluate and monitor FDA-approved metabolic medications (including GLP-1 receptor agonists) safely with lab follow-up.

Medication Weight-Neutral Review

We review your current blood pressure, diabetes, mood, or allergy prescriptions and switch weight-promoting medications to weight-neutral or weight-favorable alternatives.

Frequently asked questions

Answers from our Portsmouth clinical team about nutrition & weight plan, insurance coverage, and scheduling.

Explore All 12 Services
Do I need a specialist referral to get nutrition and weight management counseling?

No referral is needed. Nutrition and weight management are core primary care services at Hampton Roads Primary Care, and you can book directly online or by calling (757) 516-7554.

How is medical weight management different from commercial diet programs?

We evaluate the medical drivers of weight, nsulin resistance, hypothyroidism, sleep apnea, and prescription side effects, nd track your progress through objective improvements in A1C, blood pressure, triglycerides, and muscle preservation.

Can nutrition counseling help if I already take medication for diabetes or high blood pressure?

Yes! Targeted nutrition makes your medications work more effectively and frequently allows us to safely step down dosages as your A1C and blood pressure improve.

Are nutrition and weight management visits covered by insurance?

Yes. Obesity screening, metabolic counseling, and nutrition visits tied to hypertension, prediabetes, diabetes, or high cholesterol are covered by Medicare, Medicaid, TRICARE, and commercial plans, or our $25–$65 Sliding Scale.
