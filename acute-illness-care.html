Frequently asked questions

How quickly can I be seen for a sick visit at Hampton Roads Primary Care?

We hold dedicated same-day and next-day appointments Monday through Friday for acute illnesses. Call (757) 516-7554 at 8:00 AM for priority triage or book an open slot online 24/7.

What happens during an acute illness visit?

Your clinician reviews your symptoms and vital signs (including oxygen saturation, temperature, and blood pressure), performs a focused physical exam of your ears, throat, lungs, heart, or abdomen, orders rapid testing if needed, and sends prescriptions directly to your pharmacy.

Can I book a telemedicine visit if I feel too sick to drive?

Yes! Many upper respiratory, sinus, allergy, skin, and uncomplicated urinary symptoms can be evaluated over a same-day Virginia telemedicine video visit from bed.

When should I go to the Emergency Room instead of a primary care sick visit?

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department immediately for crushing chest pain, severe shortness of breath, sudden stroke symptoms (B.E. F.A.S.T.), high fever with stiff neck, or uncontrolled bleeding.
